Appendix B - Expert Opinion Elicitation Questionnaire

The following Expert Opinion Elicitation (EOE) questionnaire was created by social scientists Dennis Mileti and John Sorensen to collect information about the issuance, diffusion, and content of public alerts, warnings, and press releases for dam failures, controlled dam releases, and levee breaches or overtopping flooding in specific communities. The USACE Dam and Levee Safety Program engaged with Mileti & Sorensen to better understand what motivates people to take protective actions during an emergency, and their resulting research – A Guide to Public Alerts and Warnings for Dam and Levee Emergencies (2015) [?] – was implemented into the custom curve generation methodology described below.

The expert elicitation process involves USACE specialists in life loss estimation and state and local experts in emergency response (Emergency Management Agency [EMA]) working together to develop a consensus on the characterization of the warning, mobilization, and evacuation process for specific flood scenarios. The EMAs in those communities potentially impacted by a dam or levee safety flood emergency are asked the below series of approximately 50 questions related to their planning for and standard operating procedures during emergency. These answers help Consequence Specialists identify the appropriate warning and response parameters to use when estimating life safety risk for the dam or levee being studied.

Contextual questions 1-4 were developed by USACE RMC and added to the questionnaire in 2022. These items reduce uncertainty pertaining to severity, timing, and proximity of flooding to communities at risk.

The data collected from these interviews are scored within the USACE-developed Excel generator for the Warning Issuance Curve, Warning Diffusion Curve, and Protective Action Initiation Curve. Each default curve and information about the incorporation of this data into a LifeSim model is described in Warning and Protective Action Parameters.
