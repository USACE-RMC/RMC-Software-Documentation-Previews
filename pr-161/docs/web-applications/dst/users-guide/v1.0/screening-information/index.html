Dam Screening Tool

Screening Information

The Screening Information module records what a screening is about and who works on it: the screening's description, the dam's data, the reservoir's operating curves, the screening team, the review levels, and a history of the screening's milestones. A screening opens on this module's Overview page.

The module has five pages, in sub-navigation order: Overview, Dam Data, Operational Data, Screening Management and Screening Activity. The dam data and key elevations entered here are the starting point for the Hazards, Response and Consequences modules, and the operating curves feed the consequence routing and some failure mode evaluations. The screening team verifies Dam Data and Operational Data (see Human Verification); the other three pages have nothing to verify.

Dam location map​

The map shows the dam's location from the Latitude and Longitude on the Dam Data page, marked with a blue pin. It opens on a satellite view with street labels, zoomed to the dam. The controls at the upper right switch the map style among streets, satellite with streets and outdoors, reset the map's rotation, and zoom in and out. A scale bar sits at the lower right. While the screening stays open, DST remembers the map's position and style, and returns to the dam if its location changed in the meantime.

The Facilitator, Team Members and administrators can set the dam's location on the map while the screening's inputs are open for editing:

  1. 1The user selects Set Location, at the lower left of the map, or in the middle of the map when the dam has no location yet ("No location data available").
  2. 2A strip reading "Click on the map to set dam location" appears across the map. Its cancel button leaves the location unchanged.
  3. 3The user selects the dam's position on the map. DST moves the marker there and saves the point as the dam's Latitude and Longitude, the same values shown on the Dam Data page.

For a dam linked to the National Inventory of Dams (NID), Reset Dam Location beside Set Location returns the latitude and longitude to the NID values. It is unavailable, with the note "Location matches NID — nothing to reset", while the location already matches the NID. Moving the dam has the same effects as changing its coordinates on the Dam Data page (see Dam data messages).

Dam Data​

The Dam Data page holds the dam's identity, types, dimensions, key elevations and reservoir data. The user reaches it from Screening Information > Dam Data. For a dam taken from the NID, DST fills the page from the dam's NID record when the screening is created, and each value shows whether it still matches the NID. For a dam entered manually, the team enters every value.

The Facilitator, Team Members and administrators can edit the page while the screening's inputs are open for editing; everyone else sees it read-only.

NID values and overrides​

For an NID dam, the label of every field filled from the NID carries a small badge:

  • NID (blue) — the value matches the NID record.
  • Edited (amber) — the team changed the value. Resting the pointer on the badge shows the NID value and the date DST fetched it.

Selecting an Edited badge opens a small panel that shows the Current value and the NID value (Figure). Reset to NID returns the field to the NID value, and Cancel closes the panel. Some fields reset together: Latitude and Longitude, the dam types, and the spillway types. When more than one of them changed, the panel lists each one under "Reset these fields to NID values?".

Each changed value needs a justification when the NID supplied a value for the field. The justification chip beside the field's label is a pencil outlined in amber while the justification is missing, and a check mark once it is written. Selecting the chip opens a box headed by the field name, with the note "The NID value is" followed by the NID value (Figure). The user enters the reason, up to 4,000 characters, and the justification saves when the box closes. The same justifications appear in the Dam data summary tray. A field the NID left blank needs no justification when the team fills it in.

A justification is kept when its field is reset to the NID value or re-fetched, and it is shown and required again if the field is changed again. A missing justification leaves the Dam Data page Incomplete on the Submission Readiness page (see Submission Readiness). Writing or changing a justification clears the page's verification.

note

The page does not show the dam's hazard potential classification. For an NID dam, DST copies it from the NID when the screening is created, and a re-fetch can update it (it appears in the re-fetch comparison as Hazard Class). It cannot be entered or changed in DST, so a screening of a dam entered manually has none.

Re-fetching from the NID​

The NID updates its records over time. When the NID has changed a field this screening uses since the screening's copy was fetched, a note titled "The NID has newer data for this dam" appears below the NID data bar. It gives the date of the NID update, the number of fields changed and the version the screening uses.

Re-fetch from NID compares the screening's values with the current NID record and opens a comparison below the NID data bar (Figure). The button is available to the Facilitator, Team Members and administrators while the screening's inputs are open for editing, and only for a dam with a Federal ID.

  • NID updates lists the fields whose NID value changed and that the team has not edited, with the Current and NID values. These values are replaced when the comparison is applied.
  • Your edits lists the fields the team changed, with Your value and the NID value. Each row has a Keep mine / Use NID choice; Keep mine is selected to start. Keep all mine and Use NID for all set every row at once when there are two or more. "NID changed since your edit" marks a field whose NID value changed after the team edited it, and shows the earlier NID value before the new one.
  • The summary line above the tables counts the updates that will apply, the edits kept and the edits that will be replaced. When nothing differs, it reads "No values differ from the NID. Applying updates this screening's NID record date."

Apply saves the result and updates the NID record date and the fetch date. Cancel closes the comparison without changing anything. Applying a comparison that changes any value clears the page's verification and has the same effects on other pages as editing those values.

Dam & spillway types​

The Dam & Spillway Types section holds two rows of cards, one card per type with an icon. Selecting a card selects its type, and selecting it again clears it. Both are required (Table). The dam types decide which cross sections, materials and potential failure modes apply to the screening on the Response pages.

Table: Dam and spillway type fields
FieldUnitsRequiredDescription
Dam Type—RequiredEvery type present, from Earth, Rockfill, Gravity, Arch, Buttress, Timber Crib, Masonry, Multi-Arch, Stone and RCC. The first type selected is the primary type.
Spillway Type—RequiredControlled, Uncontrolled, or both; or None. Selecting None clears Controlled and Uncontrolled, and selecting either of them clears None. While neither Controlled nor Uncontrolled is selected, the spillway crest elevation and storage of Key elevations & storage are unavailable.

The NID sometimes lists a dam only as "Concrete" or "Other". DST keeps that value as a record of the NID classification but cannot use it for failure mode screening and evaluation, so a note above the cards asks the team to select the specific dam type or concrete section type. The page reports a required input until a specific type is selected.

Elevations are on the screening's vertical datum, for example ft-NAVD88, and storage is in acre-ft or m³. Other pages start from these elevations, such as the stage-duration curve, the failure mode pages and the reference lines on the Operational Data charts; changing one of them on those pages needs a justification there. The table has the columns Level, Elevation and Storage, with a row for each level; each level's name has a Required or Optional tag and a question-mark button with its definition.

Dam data summary​

The Dam data summary tray at the bottom of the page collects the page's justifications and supporting documents (Figure). Summary Tray describes how the tray opens and closes. For an NID dam, its status reads "No NID overrides", or counts the overrides and any that still need a justification, for example "2 NID overrides · 1 needs justification", in amber while a justification is missing.

  • NID overrides (NID dams only, Required while any override exists) lists each changed field, with the NID value and the current value, for example "NID 100 → 98", and its justification. Each justification can be written or edited here as well as from the field's chip; it saves when the user leaves the box. With no overrides, the section reads "Every value the NID supplied is unchanged."
  • Supporting documents links files from the screening's Documents to this page (see Chapter 6, Documents).

Dam Data feeds other modules, so a change here can change other pages:

  • Changing the Latitude or Longitude, on this page or on the Overview map, retrieves the seismic hazard data for the new location (see Chapter 7, Hazards).
  • Changing the Normal Pool elevation changes the default normal pool on the Stage Duration page and can clear that page's verification (see Chapter 7, Hazards).
  • Changing the dam types or the Dam Crest elevation can change which potential failure modes apply and update Failure Mode Screening (see Chapter 10, Failure Mode Screening and Selection).
  • Changes to Dam Data can clear the verification of failure mode locations whose evaluation uses the changed values (see Chapter 11, Failure Mode Evaluation).

Operational Data​

The Operational Data page holds the reservoir's operating relationships: stage-storage, stage-outflow and tailwater. The user reaches it from Screening Information > Operational Data. No curve on the page is required for submission. The Consequences module uses the stage-storage and stage-outflow curves for 1D reservoir routing, and a tailwater relationship is needed when a concrete gravity or RCC dam is evaluated for sliding stability.

Figure shows the page, and Table describes each numbered region.

Table: Operational Data page regions
#RegionDescription
1Relationship cardsChoose which relationship the page shows. The selected card is highlighted.
2DefinitionExplains the relationship, what uses it and when it is required.
3Table toolbarRow and selection commands (see Tables).
4Curve tableThe entered points of the selected relationship, lowest stage first.
5Reference lineA key elevation from the Dam Data page: normal pool, spillway crest (when the dam has a spillway) or top of dam.
6Dam Data markerStage-Storage only: a Dam Data elevation that has a storage value, plotted at that storage as a black diamond. Resting the pointer on it names the level followed by "(Dam Data)".

The page opens on the Stage-Storage card. A link from another page can open it on the Stage-Outflow or Tailwater card. Each relationship is entered in a table with at least two rows, which works like the other DST tables (see Tables); its chart beside the table updates as the rows save, and resting the pointer on the chart shows the values at that point. Entries save automatically, and any change clears the page's verification and can clear the verification of failure mode locations that use the curves.

Tailwater​

The Tailwater card defines the tailwater relationship by one of two methods, chosen with the Rating Curve and Direct HW/TW buttons above the table (Figure). Only one method is active at a time; DST keeps the data of the other method when the user switches. If no rows are entered for the selected method, no tailwater is assumed.

  • Rating Curve (the default) — tailwater elevation as a function of total project discharge. DST combines it with the Stage-Outflow curve to find the tailwater for any headwater, so it needs at least two valid points in the Stage-Outflow curve. Beyond its last point, tailwater stays at the last entered value. The chart's corner control switches between Rating Curve, the entered curve, and Derived HW/TW, the headwater and tailwater curves DST derives from the two relationships.
  • Direct HW/TW — tailwater elevation entered directly as a function of headwater elevation. The chart plots a Headwater line, one to one with the horizontal axis, and the Tailwater curve.

Table describes the columns of both tables.

Table: Tailwater columns
FieldUnitsRequiredDescription
Discharge (Rating Curve)cfs or m³/sOptionalTotal project discharge, one decimal place. Zero or greater; no value may appear twice.
Tailwater (Rating Curve)ft or m, on the screening datumOptionalTailwater elevation at that discharge, two decimal places. Must not decrease as discharge increases.
Headwater (Direct HW/TW)ft or m, on the screening datumOptionalHeadwater elevation, two decimal places. Each row must be higher than the row above it.
Tailwater (Direct HW/TW)ft or m, on the screening datumOptionalTailwater elevation at that headwater, two decimal places. May be any value on the datum.

Operational data summary​

The Operational data summary tray at the bottom of the page holds a notes box for each relationship and the page's supporting documents (see Summary Tray). Its status counts the notes written, for example "1 note", or reads "No notes yet".

Screening Management​

The Screening Management page holds the screening team and the review levels. The user reaches it from Screening Information > Screening Management. Only the Facilitator and administrators see the page; for anyone else it does not appear in the sub-navigation, and following a link to it opens the Overview page. Figure shows the page, and Table describes each numbered region.

Table: Screening Management page regions
#RegionDescription
1Access RequestsRequests to view the screening, waiting for an answer (see Access requests).
2FacilitatorThe screening's Facilitator. Before the screening is submitted, administrators also see Replace.
3Team MembersThe Team Members and Viewers, with their roles.

Team management​

The Team Management card, with the number of people on the team beside its title, lists the screening's Facilitator and its Team Members and Viewers. It manages the same team as the Manage Team dialog on the Dashboard (see Chapter 3, Getting Started). Reviewers are not listed here; they are assigned to each review level under Review configuration.

  • Facilitator — the Facilitator's name and email address with a Facilitator badge, or "No facilitator assigned". Until the screening is submitted, an administrator can select Replace, search for a user with "Search for new facilitator...", and select Make Facilitator on that user. For an Official screening of a USACE dam, the search lists only members of the USACE Facilitators group; for any other screening it searches all users. From submission on, Replace is hidden, even for administrators. The panel warns that the current Facilitator "will be removed as Facilitator", and the previous Facilitator loses access to the screening. A user who is already on the team shows the current role beside the name.
  • Team Members — each person's name and email address, a role drop-down (Team Member or Viewer) and a remove button. Changing the role or removing the person takes effect at once. "No team members assigned yet." and Add your first team member appear when the list is empty.
  • Add Member — opens a search, "Search by name or email...", beside a role drop-down. The user chooses the role and selects + Add as Team Member or + Add as Viewer on the person. "No users found" appears when nothing matches. Cancel closes the search.

Once the screening is submitted, a note at the top of the card explains which changes remain possible:

  • Under review: "This screening is under review. Team members and Viewers can still be added or removed; the name, description and Facilitator stay fixed."
  • Complete: "This screening is complete. Only Viewer access can be changed."
  • No Facilitator: "This screening needs a facilitator. Only Viewer access can be changed until one is assigned."

While only Viewer access can be changed, the role drop-downs become labels and only Viewers can be added or removed.

Access requests​

When users have asked for access to the screening from the Dashboard's Search Catalog (see Chapter 3, Getting Started), an amber Access Requests list appears at the top of the Team Management card, with the number of requests. Each request shows the requester's name and email address and, when one was given, the reason in quotation marks.

  • The check-mark button, "Approve — grant Viewer access", adds the requester to the team as a Viewer.
  • The × button, "Deny request", declines the request.

Either way the request leaves the list, and the requester receives a notification and an email. The Facilitator receives a notification and an email when a request arrives.

Review configuration​

The Review Configuration card sets up the review levels the screening passes through after it is submitted, in order from top to bottom. Every screening starts with Level 1 and Level 2. Chapter 34, Review, describes the review itself.

Each level is a card. Its header shows the level name, a status badge (Not Started until the level begins, then the level's review phase), the number of reviewers, and any configuration issue. Selecting the header expands the card to show the reviewers. A level can be changed in these ways while it is Not Started:

  • Rename — the pencil beside the name. The user edits the name and presses Enter or leaves the field; Escape restores the name.
  • Reorder — dragging the handle at the left of the header moves the level up or down the list. The keyboard can also move a level.
  • Delete — the trash button removes the level, with the notice "Review level deleted." A screening needs at least two levels, so with only two the button is unavailable ("A screening needs at least two review levels").
  • Add Review Level — below the list, opens a Level Name field, for example "District Review". Create Level adds the level at the end of the list, and Cancel closes the field.

The reviewers of a level are listed with their names and email addresses, the review lead first with a Lead badge:

  • Add Reviewer — searches users by name or email ("Search by name or email..."). Selecting a person adds that person as a reviewer.
  • Make lead — the crown button on a reviewer makes that reviewer the level's review lead.
  • Remove reviewer — the trash button removes the reviewer. After submission, the review lead and reviewers with comments in the current review cycle cannot be removed; the button explains why.

A level without a review lead shows No review lead in its header, explained as "A review lead must be assigned before the screening can be submitted. The lead can add the rest of the review team once the screening is submitted." Reviewers can be added and removed only while a level is Not Started or in its review period, and the review lead can be changed until the level is approved. After submission, a level's review lead can also add and remove its reviewers (see The review team). When the same person is a reviewer on more than one level, a note titled "Reviewer overlap detected" names the person and the levels.

The Submission Readiness page reports the configuration that is still missing, for example "Set up at least two review levels in Screening Management." or "Assign a review lead to Level 1."

Screenings of USACE dams in the national review workflow also have National Cadre and Senior Oversight Group levels. These levels show a lock instead of a drag handle, and DST staffs them from the review cycle chosen in Target Review Cycle at the top of the card; their reviewers cannot be changed here. Chapter 34, Review, describes these levels.

Screening Activity​

The Screening Activity page is a read-only history of the screening, oldest first. The user reaches it from Screening Information > Screening Activity. Everyone on the screening can view it.

Figure shows the page, and Table describes each numbered region.

Table: Screening Activity page regions
#RegionDescription
1MilestoneA screening or review milestone, with its icon, description and the user who acted.
2Collapsed team changesFour or more team changes in a row, collapsed into one row. Selecting it shows or hides them as smaller rows on the timeline line.
3Date and timeWhen the event happened, in the browser's time zone.

The timeline records two kinds of entry:

  • Milestones — the screening created or copied; submitted for review; each review period started and ended; backcheck submitted or returned; a tentative decision submitted or retracted; each review level approved or rejected; and the screening completed. An approval and the completion have a green icon, a rejection a red one, and a returned backcheck or a submitted tentative decision an amber one. Other recorded events, such as an access request, appear as cards with a general activity icon. Some milestones add a line of detail beneath the description.
  • Team changes — members added or removed, role changes, and the Facilitator assigned, replaced or claimed.

Each entry names the user who acted, except the assignments DST makes on its own when a screening is created or copied. A screening with no recorded activity shows "No activity recorded."
